Our programs and services


LIRA YOUTH INITIATIVE is committed to empowering young people to become responsible leaders and active contributors to community development. through our programs, we equip youth with knowledge, practicle skills, and values that promote self-reliance, innovation, and positive social change.

Education and Leadership

We equip young people wuth knowledge, leadership skills, and confidence to achieve their goals and become positive agents of change in their Communities.

Culture and Unity

We promote cultural values, unity, and active youth participation to build strong, peaceful, and empowered communities.



Youth Empowerment & Leadership

We provide leadership training, mentership, and personal development programs to help young people build confidence, communication skills , and leadership capacity.

Education Skills & Delovepment

Our education programs focus on life skills, digital literacy, enterprenuership, and vocational training to prepare youth for employment and self-reliance.

Community Outreach & Social Action

We engage youth in community service activities such as enviromental conservation, awareness campaigns, and social support Iniatives to promote positive change.

Mentorship & guidance

Through mentorship, we connect young people with role models who guide, inspire, and support them in achieving their goals.
